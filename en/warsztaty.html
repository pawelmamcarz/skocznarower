Dirt and slopestyle
workshops

We are running a free interest list for a working concept of dirt, slopestyle and pumptrack workshops for kids and teens. We are considering two 90-minute sessions a week, groups of up to 6 riders and three skill levels. There is no fixed program, confirmed venue, schedule or launch date yet.

What the workshops are

The working concept assumes regular training in a small group matched by age and skill, with gradual progression. The detailed program, staff, level progression and group sizes have not been approved.

Proposed format: 2 sessions a week, 90 minutes each, up to 6 riders per group and three levels. These are assumptions to validate, not an active training program.

The concept is being developed alongside the skocznarower.pl bike service, informed by Mateusz's (xPyszczek) dirt and BMX experience. Pre-start checks, repairs during training, discounts and other service benefits are not part of any confirmed offer.

Three levels

Start

Basics · pumptrack

For kids who ride confidently but have never trained technique.

  • basic and active position
  • pumping the rollers
  • balance and body work in the berms
  • bunny hop on flat ground
  • first full laps of the pumptrack

Progress

Intermediate · skatepark and small jumps

For riders who flow around the pumptrack and are ready for their first jumps.

  • manual and holding balance
  • small jumps and landings
  • drops: technique and confidence
  • flow at the skatepark
  • preparing for dirt lines

Air

Advanced · dirt and slopestyle

For teens 13+ who already jump confidently and want to seriously develop tricks.

  • airtime: bike control in the air
  • tabletop, whip
  • full dirt lines
  • trips to a bike park
  • preparing for first competitions

If the program launches, group assignment and the rules for moving between levels will be published before sessions begin.

Safety and gear

Dirt and slopestyle require gradual learning. The working program concept is based on progression, but detailed safety rules will be approved before any launch. Proposed principles:

  • Helmet required at every session, no exceptions (EN 1078 standard). For riders under 16, a bike helmet is also a legal requirement in Poland from 3 June 2026.
  • On the jumps and at the skatepark: full-face helmet and knee pads, elbow pads recommended. Equipment rental has not been confirmed, so participants should assume they will need their own gear.
  • Small groups: the proposed limit is up to 6 riders per coach, with fewer at the Air level.
  • A working bike: the final inspection scope and track-admission rules will be announced before launch.
  • Launch requirements: written parental consent, a declaration of no medical contraindications, confirmed participant accident insurance and separate, voluntary consent for photos and video.
  • First aid: before launch we will confirm trained staff, a first-aid kit and an incident procedure.

FAQ

What age group are the workshops planned for?

The concept is intended for ages 7 to 17 who can ride independently and confidently. Grouping rules will be defined before any launch.

Does my child need their own bike?

At this stage, assume that each rider will need their own working bike. BMX rental, pre-session checks and service benefits have not been confirmed.

What protective gear is required?

The proposed rules require a helmet at every session (EN 1078 standard; for riders under 16 it is a legal requirement in Poland from 3 June 2026). A full-face helmet and knee pads are planned for jumps and skateparks. Equipment rental has not been confirmed.

Where do the sessions take place?

We are considering the pumptrack in Grodzisk Mazowiecki and the Turczynek facilities in Milanówek. Venue access, schedules and off-site sessions will be confirmed after the required agreements.

How much does it cost?

Proposed prices are 50 zł for a trial, 560 zł for a monthly pass, 90 zł for a single session and 200 zł for individual training. Prices, scope and discounts will be confirmed before launch and before any payment is accepted.

How do I join the interest list?

Use the free interest form on this page. It is not a sign-up or reservation. You can also message us on WhatsApp or call 600 370 810. The /umow form is only for bike service bookings.

What about winter?

The winter program is planned as a later phase. Indoor venues, fitness training and day camps will launch only after locations, staff and formal requirements are confirmed.

Will my child learn to jump from zero?

Learning from the basics is the goal of the working concept, but the program and progression pace have not been approved. No specific outcome or timeline can currently be promised.

Can I buy the workshops as a gift?

Gift vouchers are only a working concept. They are not currently sold, and we do not accept orders or payments.
