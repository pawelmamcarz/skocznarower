Bike service for
Pruszków

The workshop at Jesionowa 18 in Grodzisk Mazowiecki welcomes bikes from Pruszków. Dirt jump, BMX, MTB, city bikes and mechanical e-bike service: brakes, drivetrain and wheels. Every day 16:00 to 19:00, by appointment only.

Work available for your bike

  • Checks and adjustments. I inspect brakes, drivetrain, wheels and play. We agree on the work and price after inspection.
  • Dirt jump and BMX. Cranks, headsets, hubs and brakes. I ride these bikes myself, so tell me how and where you use yours.
  • Wheels. Truing, building and spoke replacement. A dial indicator helps check runout; the result also depends on rim and spoke condition.
  • E-bikes, mechanical work. Brakes, drivetrain, wheels and mechanical checks. Take battery, motor and electronics faults to an authorised service centre for the drive system.

Service details: Wheel truing and building · Shimano brake bleeding · Mechanical e-bike service.

FAQ: Pruszków

Can I arrive without an appointment?

No. Visits are by appointment only, every day from 16:00 to 19:00. An online request needs confirmation by phone or SMS.

Can I ride a faulty bike to the workshop?

If a brake does not work, a pedal or handlebar is loose, or a fault affects control, do not ride the bike to the workshop. Bring it by transport or arrange collection on 600 370 810.

Do you carry out warranty repairs?

I do not carry out warranty repairs. Routine checks and mechanical repairs are available regardless of frame brand; contact the seller or an authorised service centre for warranty claims.
