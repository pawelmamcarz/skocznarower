What bleeding does

Bleeding removes air from a hydraulic system. Air compresses and can cause a soft lever or wandering bite point. A bleed will not repair worn pads, a damaged hose or a leaking caliper.

When to have a brake checked

  • The lever pulls to the handlebar or feels noticeably different.
  • The bite point changes after several lever pulls.
  • There is a leak at the lever, hose or caliper.
  • Braking weakens as the system heats up.

These symptoms need diagnosis; bleeding alone may not solve them. If you cannot brake reliably, do not ride to the workshop. Arrange transport or collection by phone: 600 370 810.

Fluid replacement depends on the specific brake manual, use and system condition. Mineral oil does not absorb airborne moisture like glycol-based DOT 4 and DOT 5.1. Water can still enter as contamination; water and overheating can impair brake performance.

The model determines the fluid

Shimano uses standard mineral oil and SHIMANO LOW VISCOSITY OIL. There is no single universal fluid: BL-M9200, for example, requires the low-viscosity version. I check lever and caliper markings against Shimano documentation. SRAM also uses different fluids: Maven and DB8 use mineral oil, while some other models use DOT. Never choose fluid by brand alone or mix incompatible fluids.

FAQ

Does bleeding increase braking power?

It can restore normal operation when there is air in the system. It does not fix worn pads, a damaged rotor or leaking parts on its own.

Do you service other brake brands?

Yes: SRAM, TRP, Hope and Magura. Fluid and procedure depend on the specific model, not just the brand. Bleeding starts at 100 zł per circuit.

Do you also adjust brakes and replace pads?

Yes. Brake adjustment from 40 zł, pad replacement from 50 zł plus pads. Rotor truing is quoted after inspection.
