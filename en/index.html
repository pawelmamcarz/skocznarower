01 /About me

SERVICE
BY A RIDER

Hi, Mateusz Mamcarz, known online as xPyszczek_. Riding for as long as memory serves: dirt jump, skatepark and MTB have been my world for years. The workshop came about naturally: when you want a bike set up exactly for you, you eventually learn to build it yourself.

Knowing what it is like to ride something under-serviced, every bike here gets treated the way you would want your own back. You hear what works, what is worth replacing and what can safely wait. No forced packages, no "recommended full overhaul".

04 /FAQ

QUESTIONS
AND ANSWERS

Easiest through the online form: pick a service, day and time, and you receive confirmation by phone or SMS. You can also call or message on Instagram (@xpyszczek_). No drop-ins without an appointment: it is a one-man workshop and every bike deserves proper time.
Simple jobs like an adjustment or an inner tube are usually done the same day. A full tune-up or a wheel build takes 2 to 4 working days. If it is going to be different, you will hear it when booking.
Yes, that is exactly the main speciality. I have been riding dirt jump and BMX for years, I know how these bikes work and what tends to hurt them. Your service is done by someone who rides the same kit.
Yes. I do mechanical servicing, so brakes, wheels and components are no problem. I do not touch the electronics or the motor, but the mechanical side is a regular bike.
The workshop is at Jesionowa 18 in Grodzisk Mazowiecki. I welcome bikes from Pruszków, Milanówek and nearby towns. Travel time depends on your starting point and traffic; plan a route from your own address.
Yes. There is a repair stand, a professional wheel truing stand with a dial indicator, a torque wrench, a hydraulic bleeding kit, a hanger and rotor straightening tool, a chain tool, cone spanners, a chain whip, cassette and bottom bracket tools.
With regular track riding, a basic tune-up every season is the way to go, in spring before you start. If you ride hard several times a week, it is worth another look mid-summer. Chain, brakes and properly trued wheels are the three things that make the biggest difference to how the bike rides.
A truing stand holds a wheel while it is trued or built. Its dial indicator reads lateral and radial runout in 0.1 mm increments. That is the measurement resolution, not a guaranteed result for every wheel. I also check the rim, spokes and nipples; a damaged rim cannot always be trued.
Truing is adjusting spoke tension in an existing wheel so it stops wobbling. Building is assembling a wheel from scratch: rim, hub, spokes, nipples. You build when there is a new hub or rim, or when the spokes are too worn to save. Truing is enough when the wheel itself is sound. Both are done on a professional truing stand with a dial indicator.
The replacement limit depends on the chain model and manufacturer guidance. For many 11- and 12-speed chains it is 0.5%; for many 5- to 10-speed chains it is 0.75%. Use a compatible gauge. I assess cassette wear separately during a check. Park Tool gauge instructions.
Yes, bleeding is one of the most common jobs here. I do Shimano, SRAM, TRP, Hope and Magura. Bleeding, fluid replacement, bite point adjustment. If the lever feels spongy or pulls all the way to the bar, it is time for hydraulic service. From 100 zł per circuit, depending on model and condition.
Shimano, SRAM, TRP, Hope, Magura, no problem. Got something else? Write, it will almost certainly work out.
