Choose the right wheel service

Truing straightens a complete wheel by adjusting spoke tension. Wheel building means assembling a hub, rim, spokes and nipples. Wheel repair covers smaller faults whose exact scope can only be established after checking the components.

Before any work, I check lateral and radial run-out and inspect the rim, hub, spokes and nipples. If a damaged component needs replacing, you receive a quote before the repair.

FAQ

What is the difference between wheel truing and wheel building?

Truing adjusts spoke tension in a complete wheel when the rim moves sideways or vertically. Wheel building means assembling a rim, hub, spokes and nipples, then truing the finished wheel.

How much do wheel truing, building and repair cost?

Wheel truing costs from 120 zł, wheel building from 80 zł for labour, and wheel repair from 35 zł. Parts are priced separately, and you receive the exact price after inspection.

When is repair or truing enough, and when does a wheel need rebuilding?

Truing is usually enough when a complete wheel is out of true but its parts are serviceable. Repair covers smaller faults, such as replacing a damaged component. Building is needed for a wheel assembled from scratch or when the rim or hub is replaced.

How should I prepare a wheel for a quote?

Describe the symptom, wheel size and any visible damage in the booking. The wheel will be inspected at the workshop, and the scope and cost will be confirmed before repair begins.
