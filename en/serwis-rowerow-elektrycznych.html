Mechanical e-bike service in Grodzisk Mazowiecki. I repair and adjust brakes, drivetrain and wheels, and carry out mechanical inspections. I do not diagnose batteries, controllers or motor electronics.

What e-bike service covers

Apart from the assistance system, an electric bike has the same mechanical components as other bicycles. You can book:

  • brake adjustment, repair and hydraulic bleeding;
  • adjustment and replacement of mechanical drivetrain parts;
  • wheel truing, repair and building;
  • a basic or comprehensive inspection of the mechanical components.

Important service boundary: I do not diagnose or repair the battery, controller, electrical wiring or motor electronics. Problems with the assistance system should go to an authorised service centre for its manufacturer.

How to report a fault

  3. Include the bike make, model and symptom in the note.
  4. If the display shows an error code, mention it in the note. The fault may require authorised diagnostics of the electrical system.

FAQ

Which electric bike components do you service?

The service covers mechanical e-bike components: brakes, drivetrain, wheels and a general mechanical inspection. I confirm the scope and price after inspecting the bike.

Do you diagnose e-bike batteries, controllers and motors?

No. I do not diagnose or repair batteries, controllers, electrical wiring or motor electronics. These faults should be referred to an authorised service centre for the relevant drive system.

How much does mechanical e-bike service cost?

The regular mechanical service price list applies: a basic inspection from 150 zł, comprehensive inspection from 340 zł, brake or derailleur adjustment from 40 zł and wheel truing from 120 zł. Parts are charged separately.

How do I book an electric bike for service?

Choose E-bike as the bike type and select the relevant mechanical service in the form. Include the bike model and symptom in the note, especially if it is unclear whether the problem is mechanical or electrical.
