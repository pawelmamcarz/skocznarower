Bike game

Skoczna
Rider

A rabbit jumps on a bike and rides eight tracks: from the pumptrack through forest roots and dirt jumps to dunes, snow and the night shift. Collect coins, grab carrots to keep going, spin flips and unlock new bikes. The game is free, runs in the browser and installs on your phone.

Turn the phone sideways to see more of the track.

How to play

Three buttons run the whole game, the way classic hill climbing games do, plus pumping. On a phone tap the right half of the screen to crank and the left half to brake, and use the middle button to pump and hop. On a computer use the arrow keys or A and D, the space bar to hop, and press Escape to get back to the menu.

  • Crank speeds you up on the ground and rotates the bike backwards in the air.
  • Brake slows you down and rotates the bike forwards in the air.
  • Carrots are your energy. When the bar runs out, the crank stops working.
  • Coins stay in your wallet after every ride and buy new bikes.
  • Pumping lifts the bike, and pressed on the downside of a roller it turns the drop into speed. It works on every track and lets you hop even on the flat.
  • Flips pay extra coins. The easiest way into one is to pump right before the lip of a jump and hold the crank in the air, but you still have to land on the wheels.

Four bikes

Every bike rides differently, so pick the right one for the harder tracks, exactly like in the real world.

  • BMX starts unlocked. Light, best in the air, slow on the flats.
  • Dirt is the all-rounder, sticks to the ground and forgives hard landings.
  • Gravel is quick on loose surfaces and handles long tracks best.
  • Road bike has the highest top speed, but gets twitchy over jumps and loose ground.

Eight tracks

Tracks unlock one by one: reach the finish and the next scenery opens up. Each one has different terrain, light and obstacles.

  1. Pumptrack, soft rollers only, no jumps. The track for learning to pump.
  2. Forest trail, roots, bumps and long climbs.
  3. Dirt jumps, kickers and gaps to clear.
  4. Gravel road, long rolling fields for the gravel bike.
  5. Rocky slope, steep climbs and very little grip.
  6. Dunes, sand by the sea and soft landings.
  7. Winter trail, snow, ice and zero traction.
  8. Night shift, riding on lights over the longest distance.

Install it on your phone

The game works as a web app, so you do not need an app store. Once installed it opens full screen and lets you ride without an internet connection.

Android

An install button appears in the game menu. If you cannot see it, open the Chrome menu and choose Install app.

iPhone and iPad

In Safari tap the Share icon, scroll the list and choose Add to Home Screen.

Questions about the game

Is the game free?

Yes, it runs in the browser for free, with no sign-up and no ads. Coins earned in the game only unlock new bikes.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. On a phone you steer with three on-screen buttons, and on a computer with the arrow keys, A and D, and the space bar to hop.

Where are the records stored?

Scores, coins and unlocked bikes stay in your browser on this device. The global ranking needs a phone number confirmed by SMS.

What do you do besides the game?

We run a bike workshop in Grodzisk Mazowiecki and prepare dirt riding courses for teenagers. Prices and the scope of work are on the home page.
